

## [bb-about] About Byte Bistro
**Category:** Company overview | **Keywords:** who we are, founder, Austin, history, coffee shop, coworking

Byte Bistro is a small coffee shop and coworking cafe in Austin, Texas. It was founded in 2021 by Maria Okafor. Byte Bistro's guests come to work, meet, and recharge. Byte Bistro's core values are hospitality first, quality in every cup, respect for guests and teammates, ownership of problems, and teamwork.

## [bb-hours] Byte Bistro Opening Hours and Holiday Closures
**Category:** Operations | **Keywords:** opening hours, closing time, weekend hours, holidays, Thanksgiving, Christmas, when is the cafe open

Byte Bistro is open from 7:00 am to 9:00 pm Monday to Friday. On weekends (Saturday and Sunday), Byte Bistro is open from 8:00 am to 6:00 pm. Byte Bistro is closed on Thanksgiving and on Christmas Day. Employees should arrive 5 to 10 minutes before their scheduled shift so they are clocked in and ready to start on time.

**Category:** Conduct | **Keywords:** harassment, bullying, discrimination, report, zero tolerance, retaliation, speak up

**Category:** Scheduling | **Keywords:** late, sick, absent, call out, no-show, miss a shift, attendance

If a Byte Bistro employee will be late or cannot make a shift, they must call or message their shift manager as early as possible, say why, and say when they expect to arrive. Repeated lateness or failing to show up without notice (a no-call, no-show) may lead to disciplinary action.

## [bb-shift-swaps] Byte Bistro Shift Swap Procedure
**Category:** Scheduling | **Keywords:** shift swap, trade shifts, Slack, #shifts, approval, thumbs up, 48 hours

At Byte Bistro, shift swaps must be posted in the #shifts Slack channel at least 48 hours before the shift. Another employee must agree to take the shift. A manager must approve every swap with a thumbs-up emoji, and a swap is not approved until that thumbs-up appears. The employee who agrees to take the shift is responsible for working it.

**Category:** Pay | **Keywords:** clock in, clock out, timesheet, overtime, buddy punching

## [bb-illness] Working While Sick at Byte Bistro
**Category:** Food safety | **Keywords:** sick, illness, vomiting, diarrhea, fever, jaundice, infected wound, stay home

Byte Bistro employees must not come to work with vomiting, diarrhea, fever, jaundice, or an infected wound. They must tell their manager right away. This rule protects guests and teammates from foodborne illness.

## [bb-food-storage] Food Storage, Labeling and Temperatures at Byte Bistro
**Category:** Food safety | **Keywords:** FIFO, labeling, temperature, cold food, hot food, use-by date, storage

Byte Bistro labels all prepped and opened food with its name, date, and use-by date, and follows First In, First Out (FIFO) rotation. Cold food is kept at 41°F or below, and hot food is kept at 135°F or above. Food past its use-by date is discarded.

## [bb-cleaning] Cleaning and Cross-Contamination at Byte Bistro
**Category:** Food safety | **Keywords:** cleaning, sanitize, cross-contamination, spills, pests, clean as you go

Byte Bistro employees use clean utensils and sanitize surfaces between tasks to avoid cross-contamination. They wipe spills and counters as they go and follow the daily cleaning schedule. Any signs of pests or equipment problems must be reported to a manager immediately.

## [bb-workplace-safety] General Workplace Safety at Byte Bistro
**Category:** Safety | **Keywords:** safety, spills, lifting, burns, knives, hot, steam wand

At Byte Bistro, employees walk instead of running, clean up spills immediately, and use wet-floor signs. They call "behind!" or "hot!" when passing with hot items or knives. They use proper lifting technique for milk crates and supply boxes and ask for help when needed. Steam wands and other hot equipment must be used carefully.

## [bb-injuries-emergencies] Injuries and Emergencies at Byte Bistro
**Category:** Safety | **Keywords:** injury, first aid, 911, emergency, fire, theft, incident log, aggressive guest

If a Byte Bistro employee is injured, they stop, tell a shift manager, and give first aid. For serious emergencies, call 911. All incidents must be recorded in the incident log before the end of the shift. Employees should know where the fire exits and extinguishers are. In an emergency, people come first and property second, and employees must never confront a thief or an aggressive person.

## [bb-customer-service] Byte Bistro Customer Service Standards
**Category:** Customer service | **Keywords:** greeting, orders, remake, coworking guests, service standards

Byte Bistro employees greet every guest with a smile, repeat orders back to confirm them, and are honest about wait times and menu items. ## [bb-complaints] Handling Guest Complaints at Byte Bistro (LEARN)
**Category:** Customer service | **Keywords:** complaint, upset guest, LEARN, refund, apologize, manager

Byte Bistro employees handle guest complaints with the LEARN steps: Listen, Empathize, Apologize, Resolve, and Notify a manager if the issue cannot be resolved. Employees do not argue with guests. If a guest is abusive, the employee asks a manager to step in.

## [bb-espresso-repair] What to Do If the Byte Bistro Espresso Machine Breaks
**Category:** Equipment | **Keywords:** espresso machine, broken, repair, Hank, 512-555-0199, fix, equipment failure

If the espresso machine at Byte Bistro breaks, employees must not try to fix it themselves. They stop using the machine, tell the shift manager, and call Byte Bistro's repair partner, Hank's Espresso Repair, at 512-555-0199. Hank usually arrives within two hours. While the machine is down, employees tell guests about the limited menu and offer alternatives such as drip coffee or tea, and log the issue in the incident log.

## [bb-closing] Byte Bistro Closing Shift Duties
**Category:** Procedures | **Keywords:** closing, close, end of day, count register, wipe tables, grinder, back door, checklist

The closing shift at Byte Bistro must count the register, wipe down every table, empty the grinder, and lock the back door. ## [bb-cash-handling] Byte Bistro Cash Handling and POS Rules
**Category:** Cash and POS | **Keywords:** register, cash drawer, refunds, voids, discounts, theft, POS, discrepancy

Only trained Byte Bistro staff may operate the register. Employees count their drawer at the start and end of each shift and ring up every sale. They must never process sales for themselves or friends without manager approval. Refunds, voids, and discounts must be manager-approved and logged. Cash discrepancies are reported immediately. Theft of cash, stock, or food is gross misconduct and may lead to dismissal and legal action.

## [bb-allergens] Allergen Procedure at Byte Bistro
**Category:** Food safety | **Keywords:** allergy, allergen, cross-contact, nut, dairy, soy, anaphylaxis, allergic reaction

Byte Bistro employees take every allergy request seriously. They ask the guest what they are allergic to and how severe it is, never guess about ingredients, use clean tools and surfaces to prevent cross-contact, and clearly tell the team that the order is an allergy order. Employees are honest if Byte Bistro cannot guarantee an allergen-free item. If a guest has an allergic reaction, call 911, stay with the guest, and tell a manager.

**Category:** Technology | **Keywords:** phone, social media, photos, confidentiality, reviews, posting

Byte Bistro employees keep personal phones away during shifts except on breaks or in agreed emergencies. ## [bb-discipline] Byte Bistro Disciplinary Process
**Category:** Discipline | **Keywords:** warning, discipline, termination, coaching, final warning, suspension

Byte Bistro aims to be fair and consistent. Most issues start with a conversation and coaching. The typical disciplinary steps are an informal discussion, a verbal warning, a written warning, a final warning or suspension, and termination. Depending on severity, steps may be skipped.

**Category:** Discipline | **Keywords:** fired, immediate dismissal, gross misconduct, theft, violence, falsifying records

At Byte Bistro, gross misconduct may result in immediate dismissal. It includes theft or fraud, violence or threats, serious harassment, working under the influence of alcohol or drugs, serious food safety breaches, falsifying records such as time sheets, and deliberate property damage.

## [bb-grievance] How to Raise a Concern or Grievance at Byte Bistro
**Category:** HR policy | **Keywords:** grievance, complaint, concern, manager, owner, speak up

A Byte Bistro employee with a concern about work, treatment, or conditions should first talk to their shift manager. ## [bb-leaving] Leaving Byte Bistro: Resignation and Final Day
**Category:** HR policy | **Keywords:** quit, resign, notice, last day, return uniform, reference, exit

An employee leaving Byte Bistro should give written notice as stated in their offer letter. On their final day they return their uniform, keys, and any other Byte Bistro property.
